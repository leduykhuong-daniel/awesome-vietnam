# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We commit to keeping this community respectful, constructive, and welcoming for contributors from different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and specific in reviews.
- Prefer evidence and working links when proposing a new resource.
- Respect maintainers' curation decisions.
- Avoid spam, self-promotion without context, harassment, or personal attacks.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that do not fit the list criteria or community standards. Serious abuse may lead to blocked participation in this repository.

## Contact

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through the repository issue tracker or the public contact channel listed in README.
